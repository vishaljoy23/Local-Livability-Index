Measures the average congestion in a city
Congestion is defined as the amount of time it takes to travel between 2 points at peak time vs at non peak time.

Method:
Plots points in concentric circles around a city.
Circles of radius 5 10 20 km
Then measures time to travel between diametrically opposite points.
Measures at peak traffic time and non peak traffic time
Averages across points.
Refer page 10 11 in attached pdf for more detailed explanation
